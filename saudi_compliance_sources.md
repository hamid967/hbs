# Saudi Compliance Guidance Sources

The in-product checklist is an **operational review aid**, not a compliance certification, legal opinion, or statutory calculation. It is designed to direct the accountable business owner toward evidence and an appropriately qualified local reviewer.

| Area | Operational review focus | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Employment relationship | Contracts, records, working conditions, and labor relations | [MHRSD Labor Law][1] |
| Working time, leave, wages, and occupational safety | Policy evidence and specialist review of applicable statutory obligations | [National Platform: Labor and Employment][2] |
| Social insurance | Ownership and review of social-insurance registration and coverage processes | [GOSI About Portal][3] |

## Use Boundary

The tool never returns a “compliant” verdict. Completing all items means only that the internal evidence pack is ready for review by a Saudi-qualified legal or compliance professional. Rules can change, and applicability varies by company activity, workforce, and facts.

# HR Tools Reference Review

## Observed Experience Principles

The reference page presents a compact catalog with a single clear promise, task-focused tool cards, concise benefit copy, and a mechanism for visitors to propose the next tool. It groups resources around common HR jobs rather than around technical product modules.

## حلول الغد — Original Tool Direction

| Tool | Purpose | Connection to the Existing Platform |
|---|---|---|
| تشخيص جاهزية العمليات | Assess the maturity of HR and government-relations workflows against the company context. | Transfers its findings to the HR-system designer. |
| موجّه خدمات الموارد البشرية | Guides an employee or manager to the correct HR or government-relations service. | Starts the AI intake assistant or the structured request form. |
| مراجع خطة الموارد البشرية | Explains the plan saved by the AI designer and highlights its first 90-day priorities. | Opens the saved HR-system plan workspace. |

The first release deliberately avoids legal, payroll, tax, or statutory entitlement calculations. Those outcomes can be legally sensitive and require confirmed jurisdictional rules and accountability before implementation.

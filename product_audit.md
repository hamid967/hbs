# Product Audit — حلول الغد | HR HBS

## Current Product Strengths

The platform already has an Arabic, right-to-left marketing site; a public demo-request flow stored in the database; employee request submission and personal tracking; operational review flows; a protected demo-request workspace; an AI intake assistant; and an HR-system plan generator. Its product foundation is therefore suitable for incremental Full-Stack development rather than a wholesale rebuild.

## Acceptance Criteria

Every new capability must include a clear owner, a role-based access rule, a success and failure state, a relevant unit test, and desktop/mobile visual verification. External integrations, automatic notifications, and file attachments remain conditional on a confirmed business owner and implementation requirements.

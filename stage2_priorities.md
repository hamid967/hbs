# Stage 2 Priorities — HR System Generator

## Approved Priority Order

| Order | Priority | Expected Result |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Rich company profile | The generator understands the company activity, size, work model, footprint, growth horizon, and operating challenge. |
| 2 | Decision-ready HR plan | Every plan includes a targeted operating model, essential modules, role ownership, and specific implementation decisions. |
| 3 | First-90-days execution | The plan provides a concise, sequenced 30/60/90-day launch path rather than generic recommendations. |
| 4 | Measurable follow-through | The output recommends operating metrics and exposes the assumptions used for personalization. |

## Acceptance Criteria

The generator must persist all new profile inputs with the saved plan, return structured output, clearly distinguish immediate essentials from later enhancements, and never present its recommendations as legal or regulatory advice. The interface must show the company assumptions and the first 90-day priorities alongside the generated plan.
